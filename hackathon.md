- Every participating project must integrate the **Attestcoin Protocol** as a core feature of their application.

### 🏆 How to Win This Hackathon

The judges and the Creditcoin team highlighted specific best practices and the 5 Core Judging Pillars to focus on:

1. **Make Attestcoin a Crucial, Core Feature (Meaningful Integration) [[18:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HPL6LjTqQm4&t=1119s)]:**

- As emphasized by Dave, do not just tack on Attestcoin superficially just to check a box. Your application’s core business logic should genuinely rely on Attestcoin's cross-chain data verification (Readability).
- Take full advantage of available developer tools listed in `docs.creditcoin.org`, such as the Proof Builder, Dashboard, and the verifier pre-compile [[20:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HPL6LjTqQm4&t=1248s)].

2. **Excel Across the 5 Key Judging Pillars [[16:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HPL6LjTqQm4&t=994s)], [[21:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HPL6LjTqQm4&t=1302s)]:**

- **Userbase Expansion [[16:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HPL6LjTqQm4&t=1005s)]:** Show how your project can bring real, new active users into the Creditcoin ecosystem.
- **Technical Alignment [[16:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HPL6LjTqQm4&t=1015s)]:** Demonstrate deep integration with Creditcoin’s tech stack and EVM infrastructure to solve a tangible problem.
- **Product Vision & Innovation [[17:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HPL6LjTqQm4&t=1027s)]:** Provide a unique value proposition, clear long-term roadmap, and competitive edge.
- **Execution Capability [[17:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HPL6LjTqQm4&t=1036s)]:** Prove the team's ability to build and deliver. The project must have clean, working code deployed on the testnet without critical bugs.
- **Market & Technical Relevance [[17:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HPL6LjTqQm4&t=1046s)]:** Align with emerging Web3 trends (AI, RWA, DePIN) with strong potential to attract mainstream users beyond existing crypto natives.

3. **Deliver Clear Documentation and a Compelling Demo [[01:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HPL6LjTqQm4&t=82s)]:**

- Provide well-structured technical documentation explaining your smart contract architecture and how cross-chain proofs are verified.
- Include a working demo video showcasing your live DApp interacting with the testnet smoothly.
