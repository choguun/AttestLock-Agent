## What this proves

The 90-second edited overview presents already completed September 5 and 9 testnet transactions. A persistent disclosure identifies synthetic narration and previously completed evidence. The player has native controls, English captions, full transcript, downloads and direct proof/lock/draw links. All six PDF pages were visually checked; the PPTX passed package, layout, reference-font, six-slide-count and editable-table checks. Browser E2E verifies metadata duration, actual playback progress, captions, disclosure, transcript and accessibility. It does not verify final production hosting by itself.

The MP4 uses H.264/AAC; WebM uses VP9/Opus for browsers without MP4 codec support. The local macOS Samantha voice supplied narration after the external speech service reported a usage limit; no new credits were purchased. The narration was slightly time-scaled to fit the 90-second overview. Caption timing is sentence-level approximate alignment, not a human transcription of live signing.
